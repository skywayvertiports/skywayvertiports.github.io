November 19, 2026 · Policy

FCC Opens Spectrum for Drone and eVTOL Communications

The FCC approved opening the 450 MHz aviation band to flight-safety data links for drones and advanced air mobility aircraft, with nationwide network licensing replacing individual ground-station licensing. [source]

November 19, 2026 · Industry News

Eagle Mountain Invites Public Input on Proposed AAM Hub

Eagle Mountain, Utah, announced a November 23 public open house on its proposed Advanced Air Mobility Aviation Hub. Residents can learn about the proposal, ask questions, and discuss potential aviation and economic development opportunities. [source]

November 19, 2026 · Industry News

Air Taxi Developers Advance Under Limited FAA Approvals

Smart Cities Dive reviews how Archer, BETA Technologies, and Joby are approaching initial passenger operations. Special airworthiness market-survey approvals provide a constrained pathway for flights while aircraft certification and supporting infrastructure continue to develop. [source]

November 18, 2026 · OEM News

Doroni Reports More Than $60 Million in Crowdfunding

Florida-based Doroni Aerospace reports raising more than $60 million cumulatively from over 20,000 investors across crowdfunding rounds. The company is preparing for flight tests of its two-seat H1-X personal eVTOL aircraft in the second half of 2027. [source]

November 17, 2026 · Editorial

Opinion: Addressing AAM’s Infrastructure Financing Challenge

In an Aviation Week opinion article, NEXA Capital’s Michael Dyment argues that Congress should authorize a federal loan-guarantee program for AAM infrastructure. The proposal highlights infrastructure financing as a barrier to commercial deployment; it is not an enacted funding program. [source]

November 10, 2026 · Industry briefing

FAA reviews proposed vertical-flight infrastructure standards

The FAA scheduled a virtual Industry Day for November 10 on draft AC 150/5390-4, Vertical Flight Aircraft Infrastructure Design. The proposed guidance covers planning, design, and construction of facilities serving vertical-flight aircraft. Once finalized, it would replace the current Heliport Design advisory circular and vertiport Engineering Brief 105A. The draft was issued October 22; this entry is dated to the briefing. [source]

November 10, 2026 · Operator News

Florida regional service plans combine PC-12s and electric aircraft

Future Flight Global plans to launch Florida regional flights in the first quarter of 2027 using Pilatus PC-12s, with BETA electric aircraft to follow. Signature Aviation and UrbanV will support ground infrastructure. The initial BETA orders cover the fixed-wing Alia CX300, with last-mile eVTOL connections envisioned later. [source]

November 9–10, 2026 · Policy symposium

Washington symposium focuses on drone and AAM integration

AUVSI’s Drone & AAM Policy Symposium was scheduled for November 9–10 in Washington, bringing regulators, manufacturers, operators, and public agencies together. The announced program covered automated flight rules, air traffic modernization, certification pressures, and airspace coordination, with keynotes from Rep. Dina Titus and Sen. Peter Welch. This entry uses the event dates; the program announcement was published October 29. [source]

November 9, 2026 · OEM News

Archer reports FAA milestone for Midnight operations

Archer says Midnight received an FAA Market Survey Special Airworthiness Certificate, enabling planned piloted operations in Los Angeles as the company prepares for passenger service through the eVTOL Integration Pilot Program and LA28 Games. This milestone is distinct from full aircraft type certification. Archer also reported that its acquisition of Boeing’s Wisk, Insitu, and SkyGrid businesses was nearing close, subject to approvals and closing conditions. [source]
